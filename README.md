# E-Commerce Analytics (SQL + Python)

## Overview
Analyzed a large-scale e-commerce dataset to uncover trends in customer behavior, order patterns, delivery performance, and payment methods. The project focuses on deriving actionable insights to improve business performance and customer experience.

## Problem Statement
As a data analyst, the objective is to analyze the e-commerce dataset to extract meaningful insights and provide actionable recommendations that can help improve business performance, customer experience, and operational efficiency.

## Dataset
The dataset consists of e-commerce transactions from Target Brazil, including information on customers, orders, payments, products, and delivery details.

## Approach
- Performed data exploration and cleaning to understand dataset structure and quality.
- Analyzed order trends, customer distribution, and monthly seasonality.
- Evaluated delivery performance and order fulfillment patterns.
- Assessed payment methods and installment behavior.
- Derived insights to identify growth opportunities and operational improvements.

## Key Insights
- Order volume shows a consistent upward trend, indicating strong business growth.
- Clear monthly seasonality observed, with peak orders during mid-year months.
- Majority of orders are placed during afternoon hours, highlighting peak engagement time.
- Customer distribution is concentrated in a few key states, driving overall demand.
- Delivery performance varies across regions, impacting customer experience.
- Most customers prefer single-payment transactions over multiple installments.

## Business Impact
- Identified peak and low demand periods, enabling targeted marketing and promotional strategies.
- Highlighted optimal ordering time windows to improve customer engagement and conversions.
- Provided insights into regional demand distribution to support location-based decision making.
- Analyzed delivery performance to identify inefficiencies and improve customer experience.
- Evaluated payment behavior to support better pricing and installment strategies.

## Tech Stack
- SQL (Google BigQuery)
- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
- Excel (Data Cleaning)
